# SONELGAZ-Industrial-Systems
Industrial internship at SONELGAZ exploring energy systems, industrial turbines, and actuators, with a focus on their operating principles, functions, and practical applications.
About This Experience

As part of my engineering studies in Automatic Control and Systems Engineering, I had the opportunity to complete an internship at SONELGAZ, where I explored different aspects of energy production and industrial systems.

During this experience, I studied energy systems, industrial turbines, their operating principles, and the role of actuators in industrial processes. I also had the opportunity to observe practical applications, which helped me better understand how these systems operate in real industrial environments.

Technical Knowledge

This internship allowed me to expand my knowledge of energy systems, turbine operation, industrial actuators, and electromechanical systems. It also helped me understand the relationship between energy production, industrial equipment, and control systems.

My Interests

I am particularly interested in energy technologies, industrial automation, control engineering, and the integration of electrical and mechanical systems.
